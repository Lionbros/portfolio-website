# Project: The Maarten van der Leeuw Hybrid Portfolio

## 1. Global Technical Requirements
- **Framework:** Vanilla HTML5, CSS3 (Modern Flexbox/Grid), and JavaScript.
- **Animation Engine:** GSAP + ScrollTrigger for all transitions.
- **Smooth Scroll:** Implement Lenis.js for premium feel.
- **Responsive:** Mobile-first design.

## 2. The Landing Page (The "Ravi" Intro)
- **Concept:** A full-screen (100vh) splash page.
- **Hero Text:** "MAARTEN VAN DER LEEUW" in massive, bold Sans-Serif.
- **The Transition:** As the user scrolls, the Hero Text must scale down and move to the top-left corner to become the permanent "Site Header."
- **Navigation:** Once the intro settles, three large "Gateways" appear:
    1. **TECHNICAL SOLUTIONS** (Minimalist/Dark)
    2. **MEDIA & DRONE** (Cinematic/Wide)
    3. **TAARTEN VAN MAARTEN** (Warm/Dutch Traditional)

## 3. Style Sheets (The Dual-Vibe System)

### Style A: Tech & Media (Modern Minimalist)
- **Background:** #080808 (Deep Black) or #FFFFFF (Pure White).
- **Typography:** Helvetica / Inter. 
- **Elements:** Glassmorphism cards, thin 1px borders, monochrome icons.
- **Reference:** mats.zip / raviklaassens.com.

### Style B: Taarten van Maarten (Traditional Dutch)
- **Background:** #FDF5E6 (Old Lace) or #FFF8DC (Cornsilk).
- **Accents:** Royal Blue (#0038A8), Dutch Orange (#FF4F00), and Gold (#D4AF37).
- **Typography:** Serif fonts (Classic, trustworthy, "home-baked" feel).
- **Visuals:** Thin Dutch flag ribbons as dividers, ornate gold borders, warm lighting on photos.
- **Vibe:** A cozy "Oma's Kitchen" feel but with professional photography.
