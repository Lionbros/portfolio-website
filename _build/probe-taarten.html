About me

Real Dutch baking

I grew up on these recipes. When my grandma visited Hungary, she taught me her apple pie. I got more and more into baking and soon started baking for neighbors and friends. That is where Taarten van Maarten (Maarten's pies) came from. My goal is to bring a taste of the Netherlands to Hungary and share my love of baking with my community. I bake everything in small batches with real ingredients.

— Maarten
