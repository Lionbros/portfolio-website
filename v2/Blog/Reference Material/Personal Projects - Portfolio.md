# Personal Projects — Self-Sufficiency & Craft

Besides my professional work and hobbies, I share a big interest in self-sufficiency. This part of my blog follows my journey to reaching self-sufficiency — the experiments, the fails and the successes, and what I do on a daily basis to get there.

## Road to Self-Sufficiency

### First Project: Chickens

A few months back, a friend offered to give me chickens. That meant I had to prepare for them. I built a coop out of scrap materials, put together a quick makeshift run, and learned how to take care of them along the way.

### Second Project: Vegetable Garden

Being self-sufficient means growing your own food, so I started experimenting with planting fruits and vegetables. Up to this point I have been successful with basil, strawberries and watermelon.

However, my plants are starting to take up too much space in their containers and are too tightly spaced together. Because of this I decided to build a proper vegetable garden in my backyard. I planned the garden out in 3D so I can accurately and properly build a custom vegetable garden that fits the space. You can see the progress below.
