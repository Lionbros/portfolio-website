# Recent Bakes — Baking Portfolio

Baking is a hobby for me. I enjoy making and crafting tasty pastries, and I love experimenting in the kitchen. Over the years I have made countless dishes — from Dutch classics to things I simply had to try once, and then again.

## The Menu

- Apple pie
- Chocolate chip cookies
- Cherry pie
- Butter cookies
- Dutch worstenbroodjes
- Caramel cheesecake
- Dutch peperkoek
- Simple doughs: white bread, pizza dough, sandwich bread, and more

And much more, depending on the season and what I feel like baking that week.

## Custom Orders

I want to share my baking with my community, which is why everything on this menu can be ordered. If you have something else in mind that is not listed here, just contact me and we can discuss your wishes.

My goal is to spread the Dutch baking scene into my local Hungarian community. If you would like a taste of home — or a taste of something new — reach out.
