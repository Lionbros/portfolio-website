Voice Cloning Experiments

Category: Software DevelopmentType: Personal / Experiment

The Interest

Exploring state-of-the-art voice cloning and text-to-speech to understand how far model quality has come, and what it takes to get a believable cloned voice.

The Work

I worked with Coqui TTS (XTTS), preparing clean audio data, running generation and comparing outputs, and fine-tuning to improve likeness and prosody. This is an experiment in how the technology behaves, done with proper attention to consent and ethical use.

Languages & Tools Python, Coqui TTS, XTTS, model training & fine-tuning, audio pre/post-processing, ethics & consent checks
