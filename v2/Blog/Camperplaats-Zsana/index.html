Camperplaats Zsana

The complete digital presence for a small camper place on the Hungarian puszta — from a trilingual website and international SEO to branding, photography and film. Each project below opens into its own article.

Section 01

Web & Development

Trilingual Camperplaats Website

A full end-to-end rebuild: trilingual (NL/DE/HU) campsite website with clean routing and a bookable, professional presence.

Languages & tools: Joomla, PHP, HTML5, CSS3, JavaScript, LiteSpeed, TLS, MySQL

Automated Multilingual Translation Pipeline

A pipeline that translates every website string NL to DE and HU in one pass, with diffs and translation memory.

Languages & tools: Python, Google Colab, JSON/YAML, deep_translator, automated QA diffing

Under Construction Landing Page

A clean, branded placeholder with an animated hero while the full site was being prepared.

Languages & tools: HTML5, CSS3, JavaScript, responsive design
Section 02

SEO & Marketing

On-Page SEO & Structured Data

Proper meta, semantic structure, Open Graph and JSON-LD schema so the camp is understood correctly by search engines.

Languages & tools: JSON-LD, schema.org, meta/OG/OPF, sitemap, robots.txt

International SEO & Crawlability

Multi-language SEO with hreflang, canonical URLs and multilingual sitemaps so each market ranks separately and cleanly.

Languages & tools: hreflang, x-default, canonicals, multilingual sitemaps, Search Console

Link Building & Backlink Outreach

Structured outreach across NL/DE/HU directories and relevant sites to win real, trusted backlinks.

Languages & tools: Screaming Frog, Ahrefs, competitor backlink analysis, outreach CRM

Local Platform Listings

Google Business Profile, Maps and campground directory listings kept consistent for local search trust.

Languages & tools: Google Business Profile, directory listings, NAP consistency
Section 03

Identity & Branding

Logo Design & Favicon

A clean, recognisable logo with the full favicon and app-icon set for consistent branding everywhere.

Languages & tools: Figma, SVG, favicon & apple-touch-icon generation

Visual Identity & Art Direction

A cohesive palette, typography and art direction applied across website, social and the guestbook.

Languages & tools: Figma, colour system, typography pairing, asset library
Section 04

Photo & Video

Photoshoot: Campsite on the Puszta

An authentic on-location shoot of the campsite, edited and colour-graded for the site galleries.

Tools: Photography, natural light, Golden Hour, editing & colour grading

Hero Video Production

A cinematic homepage hero video, shot, edited, graded and web-encoded for smooth streaming.

Tools: Video production, cinematography, editing, colour grading, MP4/WebM encoding
Section 05

Software & Automation

Automated Image Optimization Pipeline

Batch resize, WebP encoding, EXIF stripping and filename sanitising for a fast image-heavy site.

Languages & tools: Python, Pillow, WebP, EXIF handling, batch processing
