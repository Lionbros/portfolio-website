From Idea to Done

Whichever area you want to collaborate in, my process is the same. Here is what you can expect when we work together.

01

Discovery

For me understanding the problem is the most important step. We will thoroughly discuss what you're looking for and what you're looking to achieve.

02

Planning

After understanding the problem, I send over a project plan with a clear timeline, total cost, and specific deliverables. This way you know what to expect before work begins.

03

Execution

With the plan approved, I get to work. I make sure to keep you updated throughout the process and answer any questions along the way.

04

Maarten van derLeeuw.

Originally from The Netherlands, I have spent the last seven years of my life living abroad. In 2019, I moved to Suzhou, China, where I attended Suzhou Singapore International School. While there, I learned English at a native level and earned the International Baccalaureate MYP Personal Project Certificate for a project focused on promotional videography.

After relocating to Hungary, I expanded my videography work to client shoots while also building technical skills in automation, web development, and custom software. During this time, my grandmother visited and taught me how to bake a traditional Dutch apple pie. This initiated an effort to bring authentic Dutch baking to the local expat community, which grew into my small bakery project, Taarten van Maarten.

Today, my goal is to work with businesses, individuals and families to bring their vision to life. I tackle every project with the same standard of care and professionalism and aim to bring Dutch quality to Hungary through my strict work ethic and high standards.
