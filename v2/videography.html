Case study: A film that books guests.

Camperplaats Zsana. Landing page film.

I was tasked with providing a video for a brand new camper stop in Hungary. The video was for the brand new website I built and needed to tell a story. With this project it was important to convince site visitors to book a stay at the campsite within a very short amount of time. I did this by creating a fast-paced, one minute video that reflects the atmosphere of the campsite and leaves visitors with a good first impression, eventually improving the chances of a booking.

  1. 01

    Brief

    Camperplaats Zsana needed an opening film for their new website. I created a one minute video showing the grounds, the pool, the facilities, and the feeling of staying there.

  2. 02

    Approach

    I planned a cinematic mix of drone and ground footage, focusing on wide aerials for the scale of the place, and close shots for the atmosphere.

  3. 03

    Shoot

    Shooting took half a day, and was done during golden hour. I arranged for the campsite to be filled with guests and campers, to show viewers what it is like to stay there.

  4. 04

    Edit

    I cut and color graded the film in three days, then compressed it into two web versions so the site stays fast on any device.

On the Camperplaats Zsana website

The film sits at the top of the landing page, setting the tone and showing the true atmosphere of the place. It automatically plays on every visit and is optimized for browser loading speeds. I've gotten very positive responses to the film, with guests telling the owners it reminds them of their holiday and the peace and quietness of the campsite.

Frequently asked questions.

Everything you need to know before booking.

What services do you offer?

I offer drone footage, promotional films and event coverage. I work by myself, so you only ever talk to me. My aim is to provide you with the best possible service and make your vision come to life. If you have other services in mind that aren't listed above, contact me, and I might be able to help you.

How much does it cost, and how do I pay?

Packages start at €150 and go up to €900 for a full-day promotional shoot. You always get a fixed price before I film anything, so the quote is the final price. There is no hourly rate and no surprises afterwards. To confirm the booking I ask 50% upfront, and the remaining 50% when the finished video is delivered. Cash and bank transfer both work.

How long is a half day or a full day?

A half day is 4 hours on location, which is enough for most shoots. A full day is 8 hours, for bigger projects with more setups or locations.

What happens after I contact you?

Pick a package and hit Select, or just email me with your idea. Either way, tell me where, when and what you want shown. I reply within 24 hours with a plan and a fixed price, and we agree on a shot list together. Then I film everything on location and edit it into a finished video. I keep refining it until you love it.

Do you travel?

Yes, I travel. Trips within 50 km of Kiskunhalas are included in the price. Anything beyond that I bill at cost, so you never pay more than the actual travel. Most of my shoots are in Hungary, but I take projects across Europe too.

What if I want changes?

Tell me what to change and I'll change it. You watch the first cut, give me your notes, and I keep working on it until it's right. I believe that back-and-forth is part of the job and not an extra.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes. I'm a licensed drone pilot and every flight is insured, so the shoot is legal and covered from takeoff to landing.
